Common questions

Quick answers to what people ask us most.

How do I place an order?

Enter the store, tap "Add to Cart" on what you want, open your Cart, then tap "Proceed to Payment" on each item. You need to be signed in to pay.

What happens after I pay?

You'll be asked for your delivery address and phone number, or you can choose to collect at our shop instead.

Can I collect my order at the shop?

Yes. After payment choose "I'll collect at the shop" and pick up at our Ikeja location during opening hours.

Do you deliver outside Lagos?

Yes, we deliver nationwide. Delivery time and cost depend on your location — message us on WhatsApp for an estimate.

Are your products genuine?

Every item is sourced and checked for authenticity before it's listed.
